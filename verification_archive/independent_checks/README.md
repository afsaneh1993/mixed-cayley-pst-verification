# independent_checks: second, independently written set of programs

Written by the auditor; they do not import any file of the author. Group construction, Hermitian matrix, weighing test and PST test are re-implemented (`lib.py`, `wenum*.py`, `t5_*.py`, `t7_*.py`). All logs in `logs/` are the outputs of `bash run_all.sh` (about 25 minutes for the steps that existed before v12, plus about 30 minutes for the five non-cyclic steps added in v12 -- the latter figure is the sum of the `seconds` fields of `logs/noncyclic_k4_*.log` and of the re-run of `validate_prune.py`; one core). Requirements: `requirements.txt` (numpy, scipy, pynauty).

**Arithmetic.** "exact" below means integer arithmetic (Gaussian integers as pairs of Python ints, or integer-valued doubles asserted below 2^50), compared with `==`, no tolerance. Everything else is double precision with tolerances between 1e-6 and 1e-9 and is numerical evidence only. `t5_exact.py` and `t7_ab16_exact.py` discard sets whose float spectrum is not within 1e-6 of integers, relying on the integrality part of Theorem G(b) (proved in the paper). For the sets that pass, the spectrum is certified exactly and PST is decided exactly at the times pi*l/(2*gamma), l=1..4 (by Theorem G(e) every PST time has this form with 4 not dividing l; l=4 is the full period, where U is scalar). The programs therefore rely on Theorem G(e) (proved in the paper) and do not test it.

**Third implementation of the non-cyclic enumeration (not part of `run_all.sh`).** `noncyclic_weight4_third_check.py` (reviewer; uses only `lib.py`) enumerates the same sets with a different organisation (S' is found once per (A,y), independently of c, with vectorised exact integer arithmetic; c is tested afterwards). `python3 noncyclic_weight4_third_check.py --validate` reproduces 80/288/1176/2688 on Dic_5..Dic_8, none on the dihedral Z_10, Z_12, Z_14, the four Z6xZ2 cases of the log above and Z8xZ2 with y=0 (about 4 minutes); the full run takes about one hour. Its logs are `logs/noncyclic_weight4_third_check_validate.log` and `logs/noncyclic_weight4_third_check_full.log`. For every (A,y) its number of sets with K^2=16I equals `weighing_sets` and its number of connected (S',c) pairs equals `pairs_set_c_connected` of `search_noncyclic_k4.py`. Its type counts are over pairs (S',c), those of `search_noncyclic_k4.py` over sets: for Z4xZ4 and for Z8xZ2 with y=(0,1),(4,1) the pair counts are exactly 3 times the set counts of each type; for the other cases (some c lie in S') the two kinds of count are not comparable type by type, only their totals. The eight claim checks printed at the end of the full log are all MATCH.
